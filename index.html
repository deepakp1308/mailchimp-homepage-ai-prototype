Common questions.

Can I switch plans later? +
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your account settings. Your data, audiences, and templates carry over automatically. Pro-rated refunds are issued for unused time on annual plans.
Is there a free trial? +
Free is free forever for up to 500 contacts. Paid plans (Essentials and Standard) include a 14-day full-feature trial. No credit card required to start, cancel any time.
How do I cancel? +
Cancel any time from Account Settings. You'll keep access through the end of your billing period and can re-activate later. Data export is available for 12 months after cancellation.
How does Analytics AI access my data? +
Analytics AI reads only the data you connect: your Mailchimp account plus any integrations you authorize. It uses read-only access by default and shows you exactly which records it queries for each answer.
Will my data be used to train someone else's model? +
No. Your data is never used to train Analytics AI or any third-party model. It's used only to answer your questions in your account. Full details in our AI security brief.
What happens if Analytics AI is wrong? +
Every answer cites the rows and metrics it used. Hover any number to verify. We also publish accuracy benchmarks quarterly. If you find an error, flag it and our team will investigate.
Can I use Mailchimp with non-Mailchimp data? +
Yes. Connect Shopify, Stripe, Salesforce, your warehouse, or upload a CSV. Mailchimp works across whatever data you bring in.
What's the 70% intro discount? +
For new Standard plan customers, your first 3 months are 70% off. After that, standard pricing applies. Cancel anytime.
